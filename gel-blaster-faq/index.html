Does Gel-Blaster hurt?

Not really. Most players say it feels like a nerf dart, or a flick.

What should I wear?

You can wear any type of clothes, but closed toe shoes are required.

How old do you need to be to play?

The minimum age to play Gel-Blaster is 8.

How much does it cost?

Gel-Blaster is affordable, and is only $25 for a session pass with a rental package included.

If I have a Groupon, how do I book?

All you have to do is email or call, and we can help book you in.

Do we need a reservation to play?

Yes, reservations are always required for Gel-Blaster.

Can I bring in my own food?

Outside food and beverage are not permitted onsite.

Do I need to do anything before arriving?

Yes, you'll want to have your waiver completed, in addition to reading the rules and regulations.

Can I have alcohol onsite?

No. Alcohol, smokeless tobacco, and Marijuana products are not permitted onsite.

Can I simply drop my kids off?

Yes, as long as you accompany them to the check-in counter, you may leave them with us.
