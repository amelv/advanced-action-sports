The Next Generation of Combat Sports

Gel blaster combines the thrill of tactical shooting with low-impact ammunition that bursts on contact. Powered by water-absorbed gel balls, these blasters offer realistic action without the sting — making them ideal for younger players, families, and anyone looking for a fun, fast-paced alternative to airsoft or paintball.

How It Works

Gel blasters fire super-absorbent polymer beads about the size of a peppercorn. When they hit a target, they burst into a satisfying splash — no messy cleanup, no painful welts. The blasters themselves are battery-powered and come in a variety of styles, from compact pistols to full-length rifles with magazine feeds and hop-up adjustments.

Who It's For

Gel blaster is perfect for ages 10 and up. It's a fantastic entry point for kids who aren't ready for airsoft or paintball but want a real combat experience. Families, birthday parties, and youth groups love the low barrier to entry — minimal gear, easy rules, and maximum fun. We run dedicated youth sessions and mixed-age open plays.

Rentals & Gear

We provide rental gel blasters and ammo for all sessions. Just show up in comfortable clothes and closed-toe shoes. Eye protection is required and we provide it. Want to bring your own blaster? Check with staff for FPS limits and field rules. Hydration breaks are built into every session.
