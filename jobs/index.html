Advanced Action Sports is always looking for energetic, customer-focused people to join our team. We operate two locations in Massachusetts and offer a fun, fast-paced work environment with opportunities for growth. No experience necessary for most positions — we train the right people.

Game Referee

Webster · Granville

Oversee airsoft, paintball, gel-blaster, and Nerf game sessions. Ensure all players follow safety rules, mediate disputes, and keep the action moving. Must have strong communication skills and a comfort enforcing rules. Prior officiating experience a plus but not required.

Retail Associate

Webster · Granville

Staff the pro shop counter: process sales and rentals, restock merchandise, answer customer questions about gear and accessories, and handle check-in. Previous retail or cash handling experience preferred but we will train motivated applicants.

Party Host

Webster · Granville

Lead birthday parties and group events from start to finish. Greet guests, coordinate activities, serve food and cake, and ensure every party has a great time. Weekend availability is required. Energy and enthusiasm are the main qualifications.

Field Maintenance

Webster · Granville

Keep our playing fields and facilities in top condition. Responsibilities include clearing debris, repairing bunkers and barriers, mowing, painting, and general grounds upkeep. Comfort operating basic power equipment is helpful. Work is outdoors — dress for the weather.

Front Desk / Customer Service

Webster

Be the first face guests see. Handle check-in, phone calls, waiver processing, scheduling, and general inquiries. Must be organized, friendly, and comfortable with computers. Bilingual candidates encouraged to apply.

Snack Bar Attendant

Webster

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need experience to apply?

No. Most positions require no prior experience. We provide full training for referees, retail, party hosting, and front desk roles. The main things we look for are reliability, a positive attitude, and a willingness to learn.

What is the minimum age to work at AAS?

You must be at least 16 years old to apply. Some positions may require a 17+ or 18+ age minimum — check the individual job listing or ask during your interview.

Are weekends and holidays required?

Yes. AAS is busiest on weekends and during school breaks. Most positions require weekend availability. Holiday and summer availability is a strong plus. We do our best to accommodate school schedules.

How do I apply?

Email your resume or a brief introduction to contact@advancedactionsports.com with the position you're interested in. We review applications on a rolling basis and will contact you if we have an opening that matches.

What should I wear to an interview?

Casual but neat is fine — this is an action sports venue, not a corporate office. Clean clothes, closed-toe shoes, and a friendly attitude are all you need. We may offer you a tour of the facility after the interview.

Do employees get free play?

Yes. Employees receive free admission during off-hours and discounted rentals. You'll also get an employee discount on pro shop merchandise. Details vary by position and tenure.
