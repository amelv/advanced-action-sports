We're hiring at Advanced Action Sports.

Location: Webster, MA   |   Type: Part-Time   |   Pay: $15–$16/hr + tips

Now Hiring: Airsoft Referee & Party Host with Retail and Customer Service Responsibilities

Company Overview

Join our dynamic team at Advanced Action Sports, where we create thrilling airsoft experiences for players of all ages and skill levels. We are dedicated to providing a safe, fun, and engaging environment for airsoft enthusiasts while delivering exceptional customer service and support.

Job Description

We are seeking a motivated and enthusiastic individual to fill the role of Airsoft Referee and Party Host, with additional responsibilities in retail and customer service. The ideal candidate will have a passion for Airsoft, excellent communication skills, and a commitment to providing outstanding customer experiences.

Key Responsibilities

Referee Duties

  • Oversee and officiate airsoft games, ensuring adherence to safety rules and gameplay regulations.
  • Communicate effectively with players, providing guidance and resolving any disputes during matches.
  • Maintain a safe and fair playing environment by enforcing rules and addressing any breaches promptly.
  • Set up and dismantle game equipment and fields as needed.

Party Host Duties

  • Plan and organize airsoft parties and events, including birthday parties, corporate events, and team-building activities.
  • Coordinate logistics such as bookings, schedules, and equipment rental for events.
  • Provide excellent customer service before, during, and after the event, ensuring a memorable experience for all participants.
  • Engage with guests, facilitate games, and oversee activities to ensure everyone has a great time.

Retail & Customer Service

  • Greet and assist customers in the retail store, providing product knowledge and recommendations.
  • Handle sales transactions, returns, and exchanges in a professional and efficient manner.
  • Maintain the cleanliness and organization of the retail space, ensuring all products are well-stocked and displayed.
  • Address customer inquiries and concerns, resolving issues promptly and effectively to ensure customer satisfaction.

Qualifications

  • Strong knowledge of airsoft rules, equipment, and gameplay.
  • Previous experience in retail or customer service is preferred.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and handle multiple tasks simultaneously.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and a customer-oriented mindset.
  • Availability to work flexible hours, including weekends and holidays.

Physical Requirements

  • Ability to stand for extended periods and perform physical activities associated with airsoft gameplay.
  • Comfort with outdoor environments and varying weather conditions.

Compensation

Starting pay of $15/hour plus tips, with an increase to $16/hour plus tips after successful completion of training and 90 days of employment.

Benefits

  • Employee discounts on retail and events
  • Professional development & training
  • Fun, engaging work environment

How to Apply

If you are passionate about airsoft and have the skills to create unforgettable experiences for our customers, we want to hear from you!
