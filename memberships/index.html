Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the Fight Club membership?

Unlimited open play at both locations during any open play session, exclusive discounts on tournaments and events, access to both indoor and outdoor facilities, and membership in a passionate airsoft community. See the full list above.

How does the pricing work?

Your first month is just $1. After that, the membership is $49.99 per month. There's a 1-year initial commitment, after which you can switch to a flexible month-to-month plan.

Is there a contract or minimum commitment?

Yes, there is a 1-year agreement. After the first year, you can switch to a flexible month-to-month plan with no further commitment. Cancel or continue on your terms.

What locations can I use?

Your membership gives you access to both our Webster, MA outdoor facility and our Granville, MA milsim field during all open play sessions.

How do payments work?

Membership dues are billed directly to your credit card each month for hassle-free payments. After the first $1 month, you'll be charged $49.99 monthly.

What are the open play hours?

Outdoor (Webster, MA): Wednesday 4-9 PM (April 1st till the Wednesday before Thanksgiving), Saturday & Sunday 11 AM - 4 PM.
