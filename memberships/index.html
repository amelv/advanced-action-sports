Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in each membership tier?

Basic includes discounted admission and gear rentals plus pro shop savings. Premium adds unlimited general admission and a monthly guest pass. Elite includes everything plus more guest passes, deeper discounts, priority check-in, and exclusive swag. See the tiers above for full details.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my membership?

Yes. You can upgrade at any time and the change takes effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. There are no fees for changing tiers.

Is there a contract or minimum commitment?

All memberships are month-to-month with no long-term contract. You can cancel at any time with 30 days written notice. No cancellation fees.

Can I bring guests with my membership?

Yes. Premium members receive one free guest pass per month. Elite members receive four. Additional guests and Basic members receive discounted day rates. Guest passes do not roll over month-to-month.

Do members get discounts on parties?

Yes. Premium members receive 10% off birthday parties and group events. Elite members receive 15% off. Discount applies to party packages only and cannot be combined with other promotions.

What happens if I lose my membership card?

Stop by the front desk at either location and we'll issue a replacement card free of charge. You can also check in using your photo ID and phone number.
