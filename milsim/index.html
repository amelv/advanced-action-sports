Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Moose Valley located?

553 West Hartland Rd, Granville MA 01034. We're about a 40-minute drive from the Advanced Action Sports main facility in Webster.

What should I bring?

Bring your own airsoft gear, eye protection, camouflage, water, and food. Rental equipment is limited — contact us in advance if you need gear. Camping gear is recommended if you plan to stay overnight.

Is camping available?

Yes! Camping and fire pits are available for an extra fee. It's a great way to extend the weekend and bond with your squad around the fire.

How big is the field?

400 acres with 19 miles of marked roads and trails. There are two helicopter pads, dense forest areas, open fields, and multiple strategic vantage points.

Is experience required?

Not at all. Newcomers are paired with experienced squad leaders who provide guidance. If you can follow instructions and work as a team, you're ready.
