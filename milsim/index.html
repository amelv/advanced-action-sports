Military Simulation

Milsim, short for military simulation, drops you into large-scale tactical operations where teamwork, communication, and strategy determine the outcome. Unlike casual gameplay, milsim events feature structured missions with objectives, chain of command, and role-based responsibilities that mirror real military operations. Every decision matters, and every team member plays a critical part in mission success.

Team Tactics

Every milsim operation demands coordinated team tactics. Fire teams maneuver through urban and woodland environments, using bounding overwatch, flanking maneuvers, and suppression fire to achieve mission objectives. Squad leaders communicate via radio, directing their teams through dynamic scenarios that test both individual skill and unit cohesion. Victory belongs to those who work together.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Milsim?

Milsim (military simulation) is an immersive style of gameplay that emphasizes realism, teamwork, and strategic objectives. Players take on specific roles within a chain of command and work together to complete mission objectives in scenarios that replicate real military operations.

What should I bring?

We recommend wearing durable, comfortable clothing such as cargo pants and boots or closed-toe shoes. Bringing your own gear — including camouflage, tactical vests, eye protection, and radio equipment — enhances the experience. Rental equipment is available on site if needed.

How long are scenarios?

Scenarios typically run between 30 minutes and 2 hours, depending on the mission type and number of participants. Full-day milsim events with multiple linked operations are held regularly for those seeking a deeper experience.

Is experience required?

Not at all. Milsim welcomes players of all skill levels. Newcomers are paired with experienced squad leaders who provide guidance on tactics, communication, and safety. If you're comfortable following instructions and working as part of a team, you're ready to join.
