Foam Dart Mayhem for All Ages

Nerf battles at Advanced Action Sports bring the backyard showdown to our professional arena. We've built dedicated Nerf fields with bunkers, barriers, and spawn points for fast-paced foam dart combat. Whether it's a birthday party, a youth group outing, or just a Saturday with friends, Nerf is pure adrenaline without the sting.

Party Central

Our Nerf party packages are the most popular choice for birthdays. Each package includes a private party host, dedicated arena time, all Nerf blasters and darts, eye protection, and a private party room for food and cake. Add extended play time, party favor bags, and pizza to build the ultimate celebration. Parents love that we handle everything.

Game Modes

We run a rotating selection of game modes: team deathmatch, free-for-all, capture the flag, VIP protection, and zombie survival. Our refs keep the energy high and rotate teams so everyone gets a fair shot. For younger players (ages 6-9), we run low-intensity games with simplified rules and shorter rounds.

Blasters & Safety

All rental blasters are cleaned and tested before every session. We use standard Nerf Elite darts — no modified or third-party ammo. Eye protection is required for all players and provided on-site. Our arena is padded and designed for safe, active play. Parents are welcome to watch from the spectator area.

Frequently Asked Questions

What age is Nerf for?

We recommend ages 6 and up. For ages 6-9 we run modified games with lighter rules. Ages 10+ play our standard rotation. Adults are welcome too — we see plenty of parents jumping in!

Do I need to bring my own blaster?

No — we provide everything: blaster, darts, and eye protection. You're welcome to bring your own stock Nerf-brand blaster, but modified or high-powered blasters must be checked by our staff first.

Is it safe?

Absolutely. All players wear eye protection. Our arena has padded barriers and soft flooring. Blasters fire foam darts at low velocity — no bruises, no marks, just fun.

Can I book a birthday party?

Yes — Nerf parties are our most popular package! You get a private host, dedicated arena time, all gear, and a party room. Add food, cake, and favors. Contact us for pricing and availability.

How long is a session?

Open play sessions run about 2 hours with multiple game rotations. Private parties can be booked for 1-2 hours of arena time plus party room time.
