Does paintball hurt?

While paintball shots can cause some discomfort, proper protection significantly reduces the sensation. Our players can minimize the impact by wearing the right gear.

What should I wear?

For optimal protection, we recommend wearing boots, jeans, and multiple layers of clothing during gameplay.

How old do you need to be to play?

The minimum age to participate in paintball is 10, ensuring a safe and enjoyable experience for players of various ages.

Are full-face masks required?

To prioritize safety, all players must wear full-face protection to shield themselves from potential impacts.

How much does it cost?

Our paintball experience price varies depending on the day and if there are events running please call for pricing.

If I have a Groupon, how do I book?

Simply reach out to us via email or phone, and our friendly staff will gladly assist you in booking your paintball session.

Do we need a reservation to play?

Yes reservations are required — please call to reserve your spot.

Can I bring in my own food?

We kindly ask that you refrain from bringing outside food and beverages onto the premises.

Do I need to do anything before arriving?

To streamline your check-in process, please make sure to complete the waiver beforehand and familiarize yourself with our rules and regulations.

Can I have alcohol onsite?

For the safety and enjoyment of all participants, the consumption of alcohol, smokeless tobacco, and marijuana products is strictly prohibited on our premises.

Can I use my own equipment and/or paintballs?

No, Advanced Action Sports allows its own paintball equipment and ammunition only.
