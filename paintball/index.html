Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need my own paintball marker?

No — we have rental packages with everything you need: marker, mask, hopper, and air tank. You're welcome to bring your own gear; just check in at the counter for a chrono test.

Can I bring my own paint?

Yes, we allow BYOP (bring your own paint) for most sessions. Paint must be water-soluble, non-toxic, and undamaged. Field paint is available for purchase by the bag or case.

Is paintball safe?

Absolutely. All players wear full-seal masks during play. Our trained referees enforce safety rules, barrel covers, and chrono limits throughout every session.

What should I wear?

Wear clothes you don't mind getting dirty — paint does wash out. Long sleeves, pants, and closed-toe shoes are recommended. We provide rental masks and can recommend additional padding.

How long does a session last?

Open play sessions typically run 3-4 hours with multiple game rotations. Private parties can be scheduled for 1-2 hours of dedicated play time plus setup and breaks.
