Fast. Loud. Unforgettable.

Paintball at Advanced Action Sports is where strategy meets adrenaline. Whether you're a first-timer or a seasoned veteran, our indoor and outdoor fields offer the perfect arena for high-speed combat. Feel the rush of paint flying past as you communicate, flank, and push toward victory with your team.

Gear & Rentals

We stock everything you need. Our rental markers are reliable, accurate, and maintained after every session. Paint is sold by the bag or case, and we offer multiple grades to match your play style. Bring your own marker and paint — just check in with our staff for chrono and field rules. Air tank fills are included with admission.

Game Modes

From classic elimination and capture-the-flag to team deathmatch and scenario games, our refs keep the action fresh. We rotate game modes throughout the day so every session feels different. Weekend open plays often feature themed scenarios — zombie hunts, bomb defusal, and VIP escort missions.

Birthday Parties & Groups

Paintball parties are our specialty. We handle everything: gear, paint, a private party host, and a dedicated field rotation so your group stays together. Add food, cake, and extended play time to build the ultimate party package. Corporate team-building groups love paintball for its communication-driven gameplay.
