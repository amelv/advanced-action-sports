# Site Ideas — Client Discussion

## Current page structure
- Airsoft / Paintball / Gel-Blaster / Nerf / VR / Milsim — each has: desc, waiver link, rules link, FAQ link, tickets link, shop link
- Event Space (Warwick — 32k sq ft)
- Memberships ($1 first month, $49.99/mo)
- Jobs (part-time referee/host)
- Select Location (booking flow)

## Simplify customer paths
- Every activity page sends people to 4+ different places (waiver, rules, FAQ, tickets, shop) — consolidate to 2 actions max
  - Option: inline waiver + booking on same page, no redirects
  - Secondary: shop link only (rules/FAQ as expandable sections, not separate pages)
- Sticky "Book Now" or "Quick actions" bar on scroll (mobile especially)
- Cross-link memberships from every booking confirmation flow

## SEO — target New England regions
- Currently no location-specific landing content per activity
- Pages should mention: Webster MA, Granville MA, Worcester, Providence, Hartford, Springfield, Eastern CT
- Activity pages optimized for: "airsoft Massachusetts", "paintball Rhode Island", "nerf battles New England", "gel blaster near me"
- Location pages for each town (Webster, Granville) with embedded map + local landmarks
- Blog-adjacent: event recaps, field conditions, seasonal posts (keeps content fresh for search)

## Social media embeds
- Instagram feed on homepage (footer or between sections) — auto-updates, zero maintenance
- Facebook event feed or embed for upcoming games
- TikTok clips as a "recent action" strip — makes homepage feel alive
- Replace static hero with rotating customer-content (phone photos, tagged posts)

## Fluid content — no "gallery" page
- Instagram embed IS the gallery — pulls new photos automatically
- Photo grid between sections on homepage (customer snapshots from Instagram)
- Activity pages could show relevant Instagram posts at the bottom
- No separate gallery page to maintain

## Content organization
- Every activity page follows same template → predictable for users, easy to navigate
- VR page is massive (10+ games listed) — could use filtering or collapsed sections
- Memberships: one CTA (join), not buried in footer
- Footer: simplify to: locations, hours, quick links, social. Remove redundancy.

## Make it simple
- Primary action per page: BOOK or JOIN or SIGN WAIVER — one button, prominent
- All other content secondary below the fold
- Mobile: nav could collapse to just "Book Now" + hamburger
- Color-code activities (Airsoft=green, Paintball=orange, etc.) for scannability
