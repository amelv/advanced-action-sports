Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need VR experience to play?

Not at all. Our staff will walk you through the controls and make sure you are comfortable before the game starts. Most first-time players are up and running in under two minutes.

What if I get motion sick?

We start everyone with comfort settings enabled — teleport movement, vignette, and snap turning. If you feel off at any point, just signal a staff member. We can adjust settings or switch you to a seated experience.

How many people can play at once?

We have multiple VR stations available. Most experiences support 1-6 players. Escape rooms can accommodate 2-6 players depending on the room. Call ahead for large groups so we can reserve enough stations.

Can I book VR for a birthday party?

Absolutely. VR parties are one of our most popular options. We offer party packages that include a private room, dedicated VR stations, and a party host. Visit our Book a Party page or call (508) 731-6300 to reserve.

Is there an age requirement?

Players must be at least 7 years old for VR experiences. Players under 13 must be accompanied by an adult. Some horror titles may have higher age recommendations — ask our staff for age-appropriate suggestions.

Where are you located?

Advanced Action Sports is located at 109 Worcester Road, Webster, MA 01570. We serve players from across Massachusetts, Rhode Island, Connecticut and New England.
