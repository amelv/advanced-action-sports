Free-Roam Virtual Reality

Step Into Another World

Virtual reality at Advanced Action Sports puts you inside the game. With industry-leading headsets, motion-tracked controllers, and a dedicated free-roam play space, you can explore immersive worlds, solve puzzles, and battle enemies like never before. No controllers to learn — our staff sets everything up so you jump straight into the action.

Whether you are a seasoned VR veteran or trying it for the first time, we have something for everyone. From pulse-pounding horror titles to cooperative escape rooms and party-friendly multiplayer games, the only limit is what world you want to enter.

VR Escape Rooms

Work together with your team in fully immersive free-roam VR escape rooms. Solve puzzles, unlock mysteries, and escape before time runs out. You physically walk through the experience — it is as real as it gets.

Wayfinders: Escape from Aurora

1–5 Players

Escape from Aurora seamlessly blends interactive sequences with cinematic action, keeping you on the edge from start to finish. Travel to the heart of Aurora Station which is on the brink of destruction. Your mission is to navigate through the ravaged rooms of the station and save its crew before all is lost.

B-Block Breakout

1–4 Players

Play the role of a group of scallywags locked away in the infamous B Block. Solve the puzzles as you work your way through the prison's interiors to freedom. Up to four players can participate in this immersive interactive adventure played through a first-person view.

Lunarscape Breakdown

1–5 Players

We haven't heard any news from Lunar Base 42 for 72 hours. You and your team are sent to the moon to investigate. Explore the spatial greenhouses, make new friends, and solve the puzzles that impede your progress.

Espionage Express

1–5 Players • 15 Minutes

Agents, are you ready for your next mission? Your objective is to escape from a hacked train in pursuit of Dr. Montgomery. Solve puzzles left behind by the doctor, pay attention to your surroundings, and work together to succeed in this thrilling game of cat and mouse.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the VR make me dizzy?

Because of the high quality of our content and equipment, customers rarely experience motion sickness. We also start everyone with comfort settings enabled and can adjust on the fly.

Is there an age requirement?

We recommend that guests are at least 8 years old for VR experiences. Players under 13 must be accompanied by an adult. Some titles may have higher age recommendations.

What if I wear glasses?

Our headsets fit most types of glasses. If your glasses do not fit, most customers can still see clearly due to how close the VR screens are to your eyes.

How do you move in the games?

All of our VR experiences are free-roam, meaning you physically walk around in real life to move your avatar in the game. It is as real as it gets.

How much does it cost?

VR Escape Rooms are $25 per player for a 50-minute experience. Short 10-minute experiences are available for $5 per person.

Do I need a reservation?

Yes. Reservations or online bookings are required. Walk-ins are not guaranteed a time slot.

Can I bring my own food?

Outside food and beverage, with the exception of water, is not permitted on site.

Can I have alcohol on site?

No. Alcohol, smokeless tobacco, and marijuana products are not permitted on site.
